Step 1: Understand Recursive Algorithms
Recursion means a method calls itself until a base condition is met.
It’s useful for problems that can be broken down into smaller subproblems.
Example: predicting future values where each year depends on the previous year’s growth.

Step 4: Analysis
Time Complexity:
Recursive forecast runs once per year → 𝑂(𝑛), where 𝑛 = number of years.

Optimization:
Recursion can be replaced with a direct formula:
Future Value = Current Value×(1+Growth Rate)^ Years
This avoids repeated recursive calls and is more efficient.
